Hemorrhage control kit, START triage, WMA wilderness care, core care plans, and Alberta scopes.

Hemorrhage Control Kit Contents

Windlass Tourniquets Severe extremity arterial bleeding control.
Hemostatic Gauze Wound packing for deep/junctional bleeding with direct pressure.
Compressed / Rolled Gauze & Bandages Pressure dressings and securement.
Chest Seals Vented/non-vented seals for open chest wounds.
Thermal Blanket & Trauma Shears Hypothermia prevention and clothing access.

START Triage & WMA Wilderness Care

  • BLACK: Deceased / apneic after airway positioning.
  • RED: Immediate — RR >30, delayed perfusion/CRT, or cannot follow simple commands.
  • YELLOW: Delayed — serious injuries but stable enough to wait.
  • GREEN: Minor / walking wounded.
  • WMA Wilderness Approach: Long-evacuation patient monitoring, serial vitals, insulation, and hydration/nutrition planning.

Alberta Scope Overview (SFA to ACP)

LevelTypical Capabilities
Standard First Aid (SFA)CPR/AED, bleeding control, splinting, basic assessment.
AFA / OFRSupplemental O2, airway adjuncts, wound packing, tourniquets, spinal motion restriction.
Primary Care Paramedic (PCP)Supraglottic airway, IV access, authorized symptom-relief medications.
Advanced Care Paramedic (ACP)Advanced airway/intubation, IV/IO medications, advanced resuscitation/procedures per protocols.

Core Care Plans

  • Hypothermia: Handle gently, insulate from ground/wind, use vapor barrier, rewarm per protocol.
  • Hyperthermia: Move to shade/cool environment, active cooling, rehydrate if alert.
  • Drowning: Airway/ventilation/CPR priority, treat hypothermia and hypoxia.
  • Burns: Cool water cooling (avoid ice), clean dry sterile dressing, prevent hypothermia.
  • Suspension Trauma ("Harness Hang"): Gentle extrication, avoid sudden flat positioning if symptomatic, monitor closely.
  • Pain Management: Splint/immobilize, monitor vitals, administer authorized analgesia per scope.
