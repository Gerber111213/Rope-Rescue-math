Critical Swiftwater Tactics

Foot Entrapment Protocols Avoid standing in fast water above knee depth. Adopt defensive swimming posture (on back, feet up and downstream) to negotiate obstacles and eddy out.
Live-Bait Tether Safety Use an approved quick-release system with a dedicated tender watching the swimmer continuously and ready to release immediately if pinned or entrapped.
Downstream Safety Cell Position spotters, throw bags, and backup rescue assets downstream of the hazard to intercept swimmers or gear swept past the primary site.

River Difficulty Matrix

ClassConditionsOperational Profile
Class IFast riffles, small waves (<1 ft)Straightforward; reach/throw or basic wading with proper PPE.
Class IIClear rapids, waves up to 2 ftModerate maneuvering; throw bags effective; eddies easy to catch.
Class IIIIrregular waves up to 4 ft, eddies/strainersTechnical maneuvering; cold water/strainer risk; high-side boats or tensioned lines.

Standard Whistle Commands

  • 1 Short Blast: Stop / Look / Attention
  • 2 Short Blasts: Forward / Move downstream or across
  • 3 Short Blasts: Back up / Reverse
  • Long Continuous Blast: 🚨 Emergency / Man Down / Abort

Rescue Progression

  • 1. Reach: Verbal guidance or pole/line from shore without entering water.
  • 2. Throw: Throw bag to conscious victim.
  • 3. Row: Watercraft approach/rescue.
  • 4. Go: Tethered/un-tethered technician entry — highest risk option.
